# Domain Glossary

- **BCV (Banco Central de Venezuela)**: The central bank of Venezuela, which is the official authority responsible for publishing reference exchange rates.
- **Official Rate (Tasa Oficial)**: The exchange rate calculated and published by the BCV, based on the weighted average of retail bank transactions.
- **USD Rate (Dólar Oficial)**: The official reference rate for one United States Dollar in Venezuelan Bolivars (VES).
- **EUR Rate (Euro Oficial)**: The official reference rate for one Euro in Venezuelan Bolivars (VES).
- **History (Historial)**: A chronological record of the daily rates published by the BCV, used for historical charting and analysis.
- **Scraper (Extractor)**: The automated task that fetches the HTML page from the BCV website, extracts the current rates and their publication date, and stores them in the database.
- **Cronjob**: A scheduled background task that triggers the scraper at regular intervals.
